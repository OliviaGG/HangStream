How to quarry

  • Hold the mouse or a finger on the quarry to aim and continuously fire.
  • Balls ricochet off blocks and the energy walls until their hit charge runs out.
  • Each hit or explosion can peel only one numbered durability shell. Deeper bricks carry many more shells.
  • Golden vault bricks are reinforced but release richer shard caches. The five-strata tracker shows your progress toward the core.
  • Destroying blocks in quick succession builds a chain and releases more valuable shards.
  • Spend recycled shards on workshop upgrades. Number keys 1–6 buy them quickly.
  • Your workshop and remaining credits reset whenever you begin a fresh quarry.
  • Open Quarry is untimed and saved locally. Each clear reveals a new layout with 32 layers and 420 blocks.
  • Daily Sprint is a fresh six-minute, 320-block layout shared by everyone for that Edmonton day.
  • Sign in before a daily run to save your time and earn Word Tycoon XP on your first clear.
