Betwixt

Play →

Find the Secret Word

A secret word is hidden alphabetically between two boundaries. Type a word and press Enter to guess.

The word is between…
⬇ AFTER — APPLE
SECRET — ???
⬆ BEFORE — LEMON
The secret word comes after APPLE and before LEMON in the dictionary. Guess any word in that range!

Narrow the Range

Each guess tightens the boundaries. If your guess is too early, AFTER moves forward. Too late, BEFORE moves back.

After guessing GRAPE
⬇ AFTER — GRAPE
SECRET — between here
⬆ BEFORE — LEMON
The alphabet bar highlights which starting letters are still possible, helping you eliminate options fast.

Lock in Letters

When both boundaries share the same letter at a position, that letter is confirmed and locked in — no more guessing for that spot!

Confirmed letters
H
?
?
E
?
↑ H and E are confirmed — only 3 letters left to find!
Daily challenges use the same word for all players. Complete it to earn rewards!

PixeLetters

Play →

Decode the Pixels

The secret word is hidden as pixel art. Each letter is a tiny grid. With each guess, more pixels are revealed — figure out what letters they form!

You have 5 guesses to figure out the word. Each wrong guess reveals more pixels!

Read the Colors

After each guess, the pixels light up to show how close you are:

✓
Blue — Correct letter, correct position (fully revealed)
●
Green — Revealed pixels that match the target letter
✗
Red — Revealed pixels that don't match the target
?
Gray — Pixels not yet revealed
The keyboard also shows pixel previews for each letter you've guessed, helping you track what you know.

Three Difficulty Modes

Choose how pixels are revealed with each guess:

STD
Standard — Pixels reveal top to bottom. Easiest mode!
+
Plus — Each letter reveals in a random direction (↓ ↑ ← →)
PRO
Pro — Only one row/column shown at a time. Hardest!
Daily challenges use the same word for all players. Complete it to earn rewards!

Decode the Symbols

Three symbols appear in three colors. Each means one of these — but you must figure out which is which:

Crack the Code

Use logic to decode the symbols! If a letter you're sure isn't in the word gets a specific symbol, that symbol must mean "Not Present."

Deduction example

↑ If ◆ always appears on wrong letters → ◆ = Not Present
Daily challenges use the same word and symbols for all players. Complete it to earn rewards!

Common vs Rare Words

Words are split into two categories. Find all common words to complete the puzzle!

Subtextle

Play →

Guess by Meaning

A secret word is hidden. Type any word and the game tells you how semantically similar your guess is — not spelling, but meaning!

If the secret word is OCEAN
#12 WAVE
#847 BOAT
#5,201 TREE
↑ WAVE is closely related (#12), TREE is far away (#5,201)
You have unlimited guesses. The goal is to reach rank #1 — the exact word!

Read the Ranks

Every guess gets a rank number showing how close it is in meaning. Lower is better!

#1
Rank 1 — You found the word.
Low
Hot (1-1,000) — Very close in meaning
Med
Warm (1K-10K) — Getting warmer
High
Cool (10K+) — Not very related
Guesses showing "???" mean the word isn't recognized. Try a different spelling!

Think in Concepts

This isn't about letters — it's about ideas. If your guess is "warm," think of words in the same topic or category to get closer.

Strategy example
#3,400 FOOD
#180 FRUIT
#1 APPLE ✓
↑ Narrow down: FOOD → FRUIT → APPLE.
Each guess bubble fills with water based on closeness — greener and fuller means you're getting hot!

Hard Mode Rules

Rows are solved top to bottom. Revealed hints carry forward — green letters must stay in position, yellow letters must appear in later rows. Switch between Normal (3 rows) and Hard (5 rows).

Race the clock — your solve time is tracked! A new daily puzzle appears each day.

erecTangled

Play →

The Grid Grows

Start with a small center ring of letters. Find all the common words hidden in the grid to expand outward, revealing new rings of letters.

Swipe or Type

Swipe across adjacent letters to trace a word, or simply type the word in chat. Words must be at least 3 letters long.

Word Tiers

Common words advance the ring. Rare words earn bonus coins. Hidden words trigger a 5,000 coin jackpot! As the current ring fills, corner hint numbers unlock: at 25% the red bottom-right number shows how many common words still pass through a cell, and at 50% the green bottom-left number shows how many common words start there.

Final Ring

When the last ring is revealed, dead-end letters are replaced with new ones, unlocking even more words. Clear them all to complete the puzzle!

Color500

Play →

Crack the Color Code

Classic Mastermind, played with colors. A secret color code is hidden — build a guess from the palette and submit it. You have 10 guesses to crack the code!

Read the Pegs

After each guess, feedback pegs show how close you are — but not which slots are right:

Green — Right color, right slot
Yellow — Right color, wrong slot
Gray — Color not in the code
Use logic like Wordle — reuse confirmed colors and shuffle the misplaced ones.

A New Code Every Day

A fresh daily code drops every day, and it earns daily rewards just like the word games. You can also play it live in multiplayer from TikTok chat!

Triangled

Play →

Trace the Theme Words

A triangular grid of letters hides a set of words that all share a secret theme. Drag across adjacent letters to trace a word, then release to submit.

Find the Spangram

One special word — the spangram — describes the theme itself and stretches across the puzzle. Finding it is the key to unlocking what the other words have in common!

Clear the Triangle

Every letter in the grid is used exactly once. Find all the theme words to complete the puzzle — a new triangle drops every day, and it counts toward your daily challenge rewards.

Stuck? If a traced word isn't a theme word, it may still be valid — keep experimenting to reveal the theme.

Synapse

Play →

Bridge Two Distant Words

Each day gives you two distant words at opposite corners of the sky. Add words to build a bridge of meaning between them — type words one at a time, or space-separated to batch.

Links Form Automatically

Every word you add lights up links to the words it resembles — similarity is judged by word-embedding models, the same brains behind Subtextle. A word only keeps its strongest few links, so chains beat hubs!

Connect the Corners

Win by connecting the two corner stars through an unbroken chain. You have 200 words before the sky fills up — the fewer you use, the better.

In Multiplayer, every player is a colored planet building their own constellation — first to connect the corners wins!

WikiContexto

Play →

Find the Hidden Article

A secret Wikipedia article is hidden. Type any word to guess — each guess is ranked by how close its meaning is to the article, Contexto-style.

If the article is "Volcano"
#3 lava
#41 mountain
#2870 sandwich

Follow the Heat

Lower rank = closer to the answer. Use your best guesses as stepping stones — when a word ranks hot, explore words related to it until you zero in on the article.

Solo or Live Multiplayer

Play the solo puzzle at your own pace, or join live multiplayer where everyone races to reveal the same article. The weekly challenge counts toward your weekly bonus loot box!

WikirecTangled

Play →

Word Search Meets Wikipedia

Find words hidden in a letter grid — every word you can trace appears in a secret Wikipedia article. Swipe across adjacent letters to trace, or type your word.

Reveal the Article

Each found word uncovers part of the article. Rarer finds reveal more — work through common, rare, and ultra tier words to expose the title.

Name It to Win

Once you think you know what the article is, guess its title to finish the puzzle. A new grid and a new secret article drop every day!

Junk Drawer

Play →

Drop & Merge

Drop everyday household items into the drawer. When two identical items touch, they merge into the next bigger item — paperclips, rings, keys, wallets, phones and beyond.

Climb the Chain

Keep merging to climb the item chain all the way up to the Big Screen TV. Bigger merges mean bigger points!

Don't Overflow

The drawer only holds so much — if the clutter piles over the top, the game ends. Plan your drops and leave room for big merges.

The only non-word game in the hub — a perfect brain break between puzzles.

Your Library

Collect Words

Every time you win a game or find a valid word, it gets added to your Library. Words are collected from all games — Wordle, Betwixt, Spelling Bee, and more!

Level Up Your Library

Your Library starts at Level 1 and levels up as you discover more unique words. Higher levels mean more XP per second!

Level Milestones
Lv.2 100 words
Lv.3 200 words
Lv.5 600 words
Lv.100 800,000 words

Each Word Counts Once

Finding the same word twice won't add it again — only unique words grow your library. Try different games to discover new words!
