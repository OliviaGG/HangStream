FAQ Common questions
Do I need to install anything?

No. The old Tycoon Stream Bridge desktop app is no longer needed — chat-relay connects to your TikTok stream directly from the cloud. You sign in on the dashboard, click connect, and you're live. Players just open a game in the browser.

How long does streamer approval take?

Usually within a day. Each application pings an admin Discord channel — we approve as soon as someone sees it. Re-applying after a denial is fine; you'll see the reason on your dashboard.

Do my viewers need to do anything?

Nope. They type guesses in your TikTok Live chat as normal. If they want to play directly on the website too, you can share <game>.live/<your-handle> and they'll join your private session.

Can I run more than one game at once?

Yes. Pick a primary and a secondary on the dashboard — your TikTok chat fans out to both. Each game's session room scores independently. Most streamers stick with one game at a time, but the option's there.

What about the OBS overlays?

Once you're connected, the dashboard shows three OBS browser-source URLs unique to your handle — the live game board, a cross-game winner popup (1080×1920 portrait), and a rotating ad banner that auto-themes to whichever game you're streaming.

I had the old Stream Bridge installed. Can I delete it?

Yes — it's deprecated. The new flow doesn't use it at all. The download links from the old page have been retired; if you have an EXE on disk, just remove it.
