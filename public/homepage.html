How to Join a Live Game

Watching on Twitch or TikTok?

  1. Open the HangStream game link shared by the streamer.
  2. Look for the LIVE label and the streamer name at the top.
  3. Play on the game page, or type your answer in the streamer’s live chat.
  4. For chat play, type the requested letter or word exactly as shown in the game instructions.

You do not need to be live yourself. If the streamer is offline, use the solo game link to practice.

Game controls

Spelling Bee: swipe through touching letters in order, then lift your finger to submit. Letters must be adjacent and words need at least four letters.

Wordle: tap the on-screen keyboard or type a five-letter word, then press Enter.

Why It Feels Useful

A focused set of word games

HangStream is built around interactive word games that work in a live broadcast and in a solo session. The library explains what each game does, how viewers participate, and what happens when a stream is offline.

Clear paths for streamers and viewers

Visitors can jump straight to the game library, the FAQ, or the streamer application instead of digging through a marketing page.

Trust, moderation, and support are visible

The site links to privacy, terms, FAQ, and contact information from the landing page so users can quickly find how data is handled, how to get help, and how to request account changes.

Built for live participation, with solo play when the stream is over.

People can use HangStream in two different ways: as an on-stream game where chat becomes the player input, or as a standalone practice library when they just want to play a round on their own. That distinction matters because it gives the site real utility even outside of a broadcast window.

Public discovery The homepage, about page, FAQ, and games library explain the product before sign-in is required.
